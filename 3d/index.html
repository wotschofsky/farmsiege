A FARMER'S FIELD GUIDE

Grow. Tend.Defend.

Keep at least one tomato plant alive. Harvest ripe crops for points, and plant more before the field goes quiet.

Make yourself at home

WASD to move. Mouse to look. Shift to sprint. Aim down at a nearby plot and press E to plant or tend it.

Good things take 15 seconds

Let your tomatoes ripen, then harvest for 15 points. Pull weeds before they spread, for 2 points each. Clear molehills to replant.

You have uninvited guests

Press 4 and click to shoot rabbits for 5 points. Aim low. Smash moles for 50 points using E or the mallet.

Don't put all your crops in one corner

Lightning destroys a 3 × 3 patch. Spread out your plants. Lose your last crop and the run is over, even if you harvest it yourself.
